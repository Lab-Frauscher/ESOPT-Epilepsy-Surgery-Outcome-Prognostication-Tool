ESOPT Epilepsy Surgery Outcome Prognostication Tool

Research tool, not a diagnostic device: Scores are produced by the linear-SVM ensemble in js/model_weights.js, trained on a single-center/consortium research cohort (see manuscript for validation performance). This tool has not been independently, prospectively validated for clinical decision-making and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Fill in all fields and press Calculate to see the predicted 1-year seizure-freedom score.
